Learn Agent Launch with Phoebe

Nineteen out of twenty is not ninety-five percent

Every AI bot demos well. That is what a demo is for. The hard question comes after: given all these eval numbers, do you actually ship it - and what exactly are you promising when you say you are confident? This course is the launch gate. It assumes you can already compute the metrics (two sibling courses go deep on those) and teaches the decision instead: how to pick criteria per bot archetype, how big a golden set has to be before it proves anything, how to build a six-gate scorecard where quality cannot buy off cost, and how to write a sign-off sentence that survives a hard question. Three real archetypes run through it end to end - a support chatbot with no ground truth, a text-to-query bot whose answers are executable, and an analytics bot where errors compound across a pipeline. At its centre, a live launch gate: toggle levers on a real Wilson confidence interval computed in your browser and watch the number climb from 76.4% - the honest reading of a nineteen-out-of-twenty demo - to 92.5%, where it can be signed. Every metric, threshold and case number on these pages traces to a primary source.
